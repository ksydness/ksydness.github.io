Privacy Policy

Kenny's Home Assistant. Last updated October 16, 2026.

This app is used only by its owner, Kenny Sydness, to connect his personal Home Assistant server to his own Google account.

What data is accessed

The app reads and manages calendar events in the owner's Google Calendar through the Google Calendar API.

How it is used

Calendar data is used only by the owner's Home Assistant server, running on his home network, to trigger home automations. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens and cached calendar events are stored only on the owner's Home Assistant server. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
